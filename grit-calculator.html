Grit Score Calculator

Take the same scale I took in Unit 2 — 10 statements, one score, with the reverse scoring done for you.

How it works. Rate each statement from 1 (not like me at all) to 5 (very much like me). This is Angela Duckworth’s Grit Scale (adapted) — grit is passion plus perseverance, and it is not a fixed trait; it can be deliberately developed. Items 2, 4, 7, 8, 9 and 10 are added as-is; items 1, 3, 5 and 6 are reverse-scored. All 10 are added and divided by 10. A score of 3.8 or higher is in the top quartile. My score on August 30, 2026 was 2.85 / 5.0 — that is the number to beat.
